# Nested-TicTacToe

A unique and interactive twist on classic Tic Tac Toe, implemented with p5.js. In this version, each cell of the main 3×3 board contains another 3×3 mini-board. This recursive gameplay restricts the player to play on specific boards based on opponent's previous move, adding more strategic depth and complexity to the game.

### How to Play
-  Players take turns placing their symbol (X or O) in one of the 81 small cells.
-  The board where your opponent's last move landed determines the board you must play in.
-  If you're sent to a completed board, you can choose any active board.
-  The game is won by controlling three mini-boards in a row — just like regular Tic Tac Toe, but at the board level.
